Survivor Reborn

There's only one thing running through that poor person's mind...
Just don't die!!

Inexplicably you find yourself stranded on a floating pile of crates, unable to escape to dry land with not a lot to protect your modesty or defend yourself with. It seems everything is out to get you as you fight for survival.

Keep on building your raft as it comes under endless bombardment by pushing and pulling crates and see how long you can survive!

Survivor Reborn features both Classic and Expert modes in both single and local multiplayer modes!

▶ Download on Google Play

Block Lock

Slide across each puzzle to reduce all the blocks to zero, and for an extra challenge see if you can finish on the bonus star block!

Just swipe your finger in the direction you want to move the rainbow square in this completely free to play puzzle challenge!

As well as the main puzzles "Block Lock" features an infinity level which randomly generates on each play giving you unlimited puzzles to solve!

▶ Download on Google Play

Dodgy Carrot

Simple to play but challenging to master, how high can you make your carrot fly in this endless hazard dodging game?

Keep flying to unlock new carrot characters and push your dodgy reflexes to the limit, while unlocking achievements along the way!

Google play leaderboard integration means you can prove you're the dodgiest carrot out there, simply pick up and play when you've got a couple of minutes to burn!

▶ Download on Google Play
